## The tool: `ols-projection/` ("Regression as a Projection")

See `ols-projection/SPEC.md` for the full spec. In short:

- **Data**: n observations (default 20, slider 3–60), simulated `y_i = β₀ + β₁x_i + σz_i`, `x_i` uniform on [0, 10];
  defaults β₀ = 1, β₁ = 0.5, σ = 1.5, seed 4 (R² ≈ 0.63; chosen so the picture is well proportioned).
  Buttons: New sample (seed+1), Default, Perfect fit (σ = 0), No relation (β₁ = 0). Points can be dragged in the
  scatter plot (overrides kept in a `moved` map until a new sample or preset). Intercept on/off.
- **3D figure is exact**: all vectors lie in span(1, x, y) ⊂ ℝⁿ (dimension ≤ 3). `model.frame(f)` builds an
  orthonormal basis e₁ (along ŷ), e₂ (completes the column space), e₃ (along ε̂). The figure plots coordinates in
  this basis, so lengths and angles are true. The column space is the floor z = 0, so Plotly's turntable rotation
  turns the figure around the plane's normal (the user had found turning hard before this).
- Layers: the mean (ȳ, ε̄, ŷ − ȳ), grid on the plane, right-angle marks (all on); columns 1 and x, "try another
  fit Xb" with sliders b₀, b₁ (off). Views: Like the slide (default), Look down on the plane, Edge-on; +/− zoom;
  `scrollZoom: false` so the wheel scrolls the page. Axes hidden (meaningless in the slice).
- Side panels: scatter plot (fitted line, residuals, ȳ, draggable points); the triangle ȳ, ŷ, y drawn flat with
  squares on its sides (TSS = ESS + RSS; without an intercept the angle is not 90° and the squares don't add up);
  key numbers (n, β̂, norms, Xᵀε̂, angle, TSS/ESS/RSS, R², cor², |y − Xb|² = RSS + |ŷ − Xb|²); P and M with
  symmetry/idempotence checks (matrices shown only for n ≤ 6).
- Eyebrow "Econxvis / Regression basics", title "Regression as a Projection", subtitle formula; plane label
  "subspace generated by the columns of X" (user asked for capital X / "columns of X").

## Gotchas learned

- Plotly 3D: equal scaling needs `aspectmode: 'manual'` with `aspectratio` proportional to the axis ranges.
  Camera `eye` is in normalized scene coordinates; the frame keeps ŷ along +x so the camera presets are constants.
- `uirevision: 'keep'` keeps the user's camera; `plotly_relayout` with `scene.camera` switches the view to "free".
- Arrowheads are `cone` traces with `sizemode: 'absolute'`, `anchor: 'tip'`, `sizeref` ≈ 3.5% of the scene size.
- 3D text labels are clipped outside the axis ranges; keep labels inside the box. The plane label is hidden in
  Edge-on view (it overlaps the origin there).
- Rounding noise (1e-16) must be shown as 0 (`clean` / `f2` in app.js).
- On narrow screens `fitToPanel` moves the camera back so the figure is not cut off.
