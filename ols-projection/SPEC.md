# Regression as a Projection — specification

Lecture: Lecture 1, *Regression basics* (handout `econx_1`), slides "Geometry: It is a simple projection." and "Projection".
Goal: make the hand-drawn figure of the slide interactive: least squares is the orthogonal projection of $y$ onto the
subspace generated by the columns of $X$, and $TSS = ESS + RSS$ is Pythagoras in the triangle $\bar y$, $\hat y$, $y$.

## Status

- Built: `model.js` (tested, `node ols-projection/test-model.cjs`), `index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`.

## Model (`model.js`, `window.ProjectionModel`)

$n = 3$ observations so that $y \in \mathbb R^3$ can be drawn. Columns of $X$: $\mathbf 1$ and $x$ (with an intercept) or $x$ alone.
The projection is computed from an orthonormal basis of the column space (Gram–Schmidt), so $\hat y$ and $\hat\varepsilon$
exist even when the columns are collinear and $\hat\beta$ is not unique (then `beta` is `null`).

`fit(x, y, intercept)` returns `cols, basis, rank, beta, yhat, resid, ybar, ebar, explained, tss, ess, rss, r2, r2ess, r2cor, P, M, XtE, normal, onesIn, angleResid`.
Helpers: `design, fitted, rss, basis, project, projectionMatrix, residualMaker, normalEquations, simpleOLS` and small vector/matrix functions.

Tests check, on fixed and random data with and without an intercept: $\hat\beta$ against brute-force minimisation of RSS and
against $S_{xy}/S_{xx}$; $\hat y = X\hat\beta = Py$ with the explicit $P = X(X^TX)^{-1}X^T$; $P, M$ symmetric and idempotent, $PM=0$,
trace $P$ = rank; $X^T\hat\varepsilon = 0$; $|y|^2 = |\hat y|^2 + |\hat\varepsilon|^2$; $RSS(b) = RSS + |X(\hat\beta - b)|^2$;
with an intercept $TSS = ESS + RSS$ and $R^2 = 1-RSS/TSS = ESS/TSS = \mathrm{cor}(\hat y, y)^2$; without an intercept the
decomposition fails in general; the collinear case.

### Controls
1. How to read this.
2. Model: intercept on/off; the model and the numbers $y$, $X$, $\hat\beta$ in KaTeX.
3. Data: sliders for $y_1, y_2, y_3$ and $x_1, x_2, x_3$ (−2 to 6); examples: default (as on the slide: $\hat\beta = (0.5, 1)$, ESS = 2, RSS = 1.5), perfect fit, $R^2 = 0$, $x$ constant (collinear).
4. Layers: the mean ($\bar y$, $\bar\varepsilon$, $\hat y - \bar y$), columns of $X$, right-angle marks, "try another fit $Xb$" with sliders $b_0, b_1$ and a button $b = \hat\beta$.

### 3D figure (Plotly)
The column space as a shaded plane (a line when the columns are collinear or there is no intercept), the origin, arrows
$y$ (black), $\hat y$ (blue), $\hat\varepsilon$ (red, from $\hat y$ to $y$), $\bar y$ (green), $\bar\varepsilon$ (yellow, from $\bar y$ to $y$),
the segment $\hat y - \bar y$ (purple), the columns $\mathbf 1$ and $x$ (grey), a right-angle mark at $\hat y$, and optionally $Xb$
with the dashed distance $y - Xb$. The scene is a cube, so right angles look right.
Views: **Like the slide** (plane level, residual up, $\hat y$ to the right), **Look down on the plane** ($y$ lands on $\hat y$),
**Edge-on** (the plane is a line, $\hat\varepsilon$ perpendicular to it), **Axes** (the coordinates are the observations $i = 1, 2, 3$).
The views follow the data until the user rotates the figure.

### Side panels
- Scatter plot of $(x_i, y_i)$ with the fitted line, $\bar y$, and per observation the vertical pieces $y_i - \bar y$ (yellow) =
  $\hat y_i - \bar y$ (purple) + $y_i - \hat y_i$ (red). Points can be dragged.
- The triangle $\bar y$, $\hat y$, $y$ drawn flat with true side lengths and squares on its sides: TSS = ESS + RSS.
  Without an intercept the angle at $\hat y$ is not 90° and the squares do not add up.
- Key numbers: $\hat\beta$, $\hat y$, $\hat\varepsilon$, $X^T\hat\varepsilon$, angle between $\hat y$ and $\hat\varepsilon$, TSS, ESS, RSS,
  $R^2$, $\mathrm{cor}(\hat y, y)^2$, $|y - Xb|^2 = RSS + |\hat y - Xb|^2$; a one-sentence reading; $P$ and $M$ with checks of symmetry and idempotence.
