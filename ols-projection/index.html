How to read this

Stack the observations of the dependent variable into one vector . Each column of is a vector in the same space, and every possible fit is a combination of these columns: together they form the subspace generated by the x's, here a plane. With observations we can draw it.

Least squares picks the point of the plane that is closest to , because the sum of squares is the squared distance between them. The closest point is the foot of the perpendicular: is the orthogonal projection of , and the residual sticks straight out of the plane.

With an intercept, the vector of means lies in the plane too, so , and form a right triangle: is Pythagoras.
